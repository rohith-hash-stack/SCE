# Prototype P1: payload compression with v2 summaries

**Outcome: A1 passes P1** (`reports/retrieval_pivot/p1/NOTES.md`): more gold present in 25-30% fewer
tokens at every budget, summaries <= 6.5% of the payload. The gain comes from compression; A1n (no
summary) matches it, so the summary's value is P3's question.

Offline, no model. Code: `p1.py`; tests: `tests/test_p1_payload.py`. Design context:
`docs/retrieval_pivot_investigation.md`, section 6.

## Question

When a slice's outer nodes are sent as signature + contract + v2 summary instead of their source,
do more of the nodes a task needs fit within 2k / 4k / 8k tokens, and at what cost in fidelity and noise?

## Representations and arms

The seed (anchor) is always sent in full, as the packer sends it today.
Ring = hops from the seed over the graph's traversable edges.

| arm | ring 1 (immediate helpers) | ring >= 2 (outer) | tests |
|---|---|---|---|
| A0 | full | full | baseline |
| A1 | full | sig + contract + summary | outer compression |
| A1n | full | sig + contract | control: A1 without the summary |
| A2 | sig + contract + summary | sig + contract + summary | compressing the immediate helpers too |
| A3 | full + summary | sig + contract + summary | enriching helpers with their summary |

Selection is the production packer (`pack_symbol_context`, called as `prism.surface.build` calls it).
Costs are re-priced per arm (`_default_costs` patched for the call): a node costs what it is sent
as. Every arm is rendered by `p1.Renderer` and token-counted the same way.

Deviation from production, by design: production prices bodies only and trims at render time. P1
prices the rendered text, so A0 is P1's own baseline. Its selection overlaps production's at a
mean Jaccard of 0.71 (`reports/retrieval_pivot/p1/dry_no_summaries`).

## Gold (Prism never defines it)

- `q2e/*` (21 cases): query_to_evidence requirements - seed = gold subject, gold nodes =
  required symbols, gold edges = call-site relationship paths.
- `l4/*` (8 cases): Layer 4 runtime traces on Django and FastAPI - seed = focal, gold nodes =
  repo functions observed at call depth <= 2 above or below it.
- 29 cases, 204 gold nodes, 70 gold edges.

## Summaries

The locked v2 summarizer, on just the 552 functions the packer can choose from in any case
(`needed_<corpus>.txt`; `prism summarize --only`): `kaggle/p1_summaries_cell.py` ->
`reports/retrieval_pivot/p1_summaries/<corpus>.json`.

## Metrics

Per case, arm and budget:
- tokens used, and nodes by representation;
- gold nodes present (any representation, at full body, only compressed);
- gold edges with both ends present;
- noise share: tokens on nodes that are neither seed nor gold;
- summary tokens;
- the ceiling: gold inside the packer's candidate pool at all.

## Criteria (fixed before any summary result)

- **A1 passes P1** if, pooled over the 29 cases at every budget, it has gold present >= A0 and
  tokens <= A0, and its summary tokens are <= 15% of its payload.
- **A2 vs A1**: the gold gained by compressing helpers, against the gold that drops from full body
  to compressed-only. This is the fidelity cost the anchor and helpers rule guards against;
  reported, not gated.
- **A3 vs A1**: the tokens enrichment adds, against gold present (noise). Reported.
- **P1 cannot measure understanding or hallucination.** A1 and A1n select almost the same nodes
  and differ in the summary text. Whether that text helps or misleads a model is P3's test: the
  same payloads to a fixed host model, graded by Layer 4 execution and Q2E grounding, under a
  rule fixed before it runs. P3 runs only if A1 passes P1.
