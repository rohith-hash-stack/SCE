# Phase P3: the payload arms in front of a model

**Status: P3a done** (`reports/retrieval_pivot/p3a/NOTES.md`: exploratory, decides nothing).
**P3b revised after P3a** (section "P3b blueprint"): the baseline is now `prism_full`, the arms are
R1_A0 and R1_A1n, and backward tasks are secondary. Two choices await approval before the tasks are
frozen: the margins and the task count. No P3b task exists yet.

## Arms (`p3.py`)

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R0_A0 | production packer | full | full | full | A0 |
| R1_A0 | + ring fill | full | full | full | A0 |
| R1_A1n | + ring fill | full | full | signature + contract | A1n |
| R1_A4 | + ring fill | full | full + 4-axis profile | full | A6 (new) |

## Grading

- **Forward blast radius (primary).** The tournament's `score_forward`: precision, recall and F1 of
  the affected functions the model names, against runtime-traced callers (Django: tables and
  side-effect tags too). It discriminates: in the tournament, mean F1 was 0.07 with no context,
  0.50 for `prism_full` and 0.78 with an oracle context.
- **Fabrication (secondary).** Named symbols that are not in the corpus index at all - the
  hallucination measure, from the same reply.
- **Backward contract adaptation (reported, not decisive).** Splice the reply and run the corpus's
  gating tests. In the tournament every arm, the oracle included, passed 0-3% (0 of 80 with oracle
  context): a floor effect at this model size. It cannot rank five arms.
- **Q2E grounding (deferred).** Its grader counts a claim as supported only when a full caller body
  showing the call was delivered, so it penalizes signature-compressed arms by construction. It also
  grades stored T2/T5 answers, which P3 would first have to generate. A fair version needs a rule
  for compressed evidence, decided before any P3 answer exists.

## The task-set problem

The 16 Layer 4 tasks (8 forward, 8 backward; Django, FastAPI, Express, tRPC) are what P3 can run
on today, but:
- only the 8 forward tasks can discriminate;
- the 4 Python forward tasks are among the 29 cases P1 used to design R1, so they are not held
  out for R1;
- 8 tasks are few: a two-sided sign test over tasks needs 7 / 1 to reach p < 0.05.

Proposal:

- **P3a, exploratory**: the 16 existing tasks x 10 arms x 10 seeds (1,600 cells). It reports
  everything and decides nothing.
- **P3b, confirmatory**: at least 24 new forward tasks, authored with `layer4/author.py` and traced
  on fresh focal symbols across the four corpora, frozen before any P3 cell runs. The rule below
  applies to P3b only.

## P3b blueprint (revised after P3a)

### What P3a changed

- R0_A0 (P3's one-pass rendering of the production pack) scored F1 0.36. The shipped two-pass
  `prism_full` scored 0.50 on the same tasks, model and seeds in the tournament. Beating R0_A0
  would not show an improvement over Prism today, so `prism_full` is the baseline.
- R1_A0 matched `prism_full` on P3a (0.477 vs 0.496; 4 tasks better, 4 worse) with one model call
  instead of two. The question becomes: does one-pass R1 match `prism_full`?
- Outer-node signatures (R1_A1n) kept mean F1 (0.478 vs 0.477) in 29% fewer tokens. But they lost
  on a code-heavy task (django_02: 0.74 -> 0.60) and passed no backward task (0 / 80, vs 12 / 80
  for R1_A0). So they are a token saving with a fidelity cost, tested as such.
- The 4-axis profile arms (R1_A4, R1_A5) are dropped: equal or lower F1 than their counterparts.

### Arms (all at 8k, in the same sweep, same code)

| arm | what it is | model calls per cell |
|---|---|---|
| `prism_full` (baseline) | the tournament arm, unchanged: Turn 1 selects from a manifest, Turn 2 answers | 2 |
| `p3_R1_A0@8k` | production pack + R1 ring fill, full code | 1 |
| `p3_R1_A1n@8k` | the same, outer nodes (>= 2 hops) as signature + contract | 1 |
| `prism_oracle` (diagnostic, recommended) | the oracle context: the headroom (0.78 on P3a's tasks) | 1 |

`prism_full` is run again in P3b, not reused from the tournament: the same session, code and
environment as the other arms.

### Tasks

- **Primary: new forward tasks.** At least 24, recommended **32: 8 per corpus** (Django, FastAPI,
  Express, tRPC); see "Power" below. Authored, traced and frozen before any P3b cell runs (plan
  below).
- **Secondary: backward adaptation.** The 8 existing backward tasks. They were in P3a, so this is
  a tracked signal, not a held-out test.

### Grading

- **Primary:** forward F1 per task = mean over 10 seeds (42-51), against the runtime-traced callers
  (`tasks.score_forward`, unchanged).
- **Disqualifier:** fabrication rate (named symbols not in the corpus index, over all names) more
  than 0.05 above `prism_full`'s.
- **Secondary, reported, not tested:** backward pass rate per arm; precision and recall; Django
  tables and side-effect tags; context tokens; total prompt tokens (Turn 1 included) and model calls.

### Pre-registered rule (P3b)

"Matches" needs a margin on the size of the difference, which a sign test cannot give, so both
steps are non-inferiority tests on paired per-task differences.

- Interval: the 95% paired bootstrap interval of the mean per-task F1 difference (candidate minus
  reference): 10,000 resamples of tasks, fixed seed 0. Sign test and per-task table reported alongside.
- Fixed sequence, stopping at the first failure:
  1. **Match the baseline.** R1_A0 vs `prism_full`: the interval's lower bound > -delta1, and not
     disqualified. If the lower bound is also > 0, R1_A0 is reported as **beating** `prism_full`.
  2. **Compression costs nothing.** R1_A1n vs R1_A0: the lower bound > -delta2, and not
     disqualified.
- **Outcome:**
  - step 1 fails: `prism_full` stays; R1 is not adopted;
  - step 1 passes and step 2 fails: R1_A0 is adopted - one model call fewer at matched F1;
  - both pass: R1_A1n is adopted for forward / blast-radius payloads. If its backward pass count is
    below R1_A0's, implementation payloads keep full code (R1_A0). This is a shipping rule on a
    secondary signal, not a statistical test.
- **Margins (to approve):** delta1 = **0.075** (15% of `prism_full`'s 0.50), delta2 = **0.04**.
- Shipping means implementing R1 (and A1n, if adopted) in the production packer and renderer,
  with their own tests. P3b does not ship code.

### Power (from P3a's per-task differences)

| tasks | R1_A0 - prism_full: expected lower bound | R1_A1n - R1_A0: expected lower bound |
|---|---|---|
| 24 | -0.074 | -0.032 |
| 32 | -0.066 | -0.028 |
| 48 | -0.058 | -0.022 |

P3a's differences: mean -0.019 and SD 0.136 for R1_A0 - `prism_full`; mean +0.002 and SD 0.084
for R1_A1n - R1_A0. Seed noise inside a task is SD 0.095 over 10 seeds; between tasks it
dominates. If P3a's means hold, 32 tasks put the expected lower bounds at -0.066 > -0.075 and
-0.028 > -0.04, with modest room. 24 tasks would leave step 1 on its edge (-0.074). A tighter
delta1 of 0.05 needs roughly 64 tasks.

### Task authoring and freezing (the P3b prerequisite)

Gold must come from runtime traces, never from Prism, and the focal symbols must be chosen without
looking at anything an arm uses.

1. **Draw focal candidates (Prism-neutral).** Per corpus, list the indexed library functions and
   methods, outside test files and the example zone (`author.py`'s own rule). Excluded:
   - every focal and gold symbol of the 16 existing Layer 4 tasks;
   - every query_to_evidence subject and required symbol;
   - every seed of P1's 29 cases.

   Shuffle with a fixed seed (recorded) and take candidates in that order. No graph property
   (callers, degree, reach) is used in the draw, so the draw cannot favour R1. Script:
   `p3b_draw.py`, committed with its output before any trace.
2. **Write the specs by hand, in draw order.** The change description is a signature change, as
   in the existing tasks. It must not name any caller (the authoring prompt-leak check), and the
   trace labels are the focal's own test modules. File: `benchmarks/layer4/specs_p3b.py`.
3. **Trace and derive gold** with `author.py` (a `--specs` option for the new file), on Kaggle, where
   the corpora's test environments exist (`ENVIRONMENT.md`; a cell based on the tournament and
   tRPC authoring cells). Eligibility, decided by the trace alone:
   - the trace exits 0;
   - the focal runs (`focal_calls` >= 1);
   - 3 to 15 gold callers within `max_depth` 2.

   An ineligible candidate is skipped, logged and replaced by the next in draw order, until 8 per
   corpus.
4. **Freeze.** Tasks and manifests go to `benchmarks/layer4_p3b/` with a `FROZEN.json`: the
   sha256 of every task file, the draw seed, and the skipped candidates with their reasons. It is
   committed before any P3b cell. The P3b cell refuses to run unless every hash matches. No
   Prism context, search or model call touches the new tasks before the freeze.
5. **Run P3b.** A cell derived from `kaggle/p3a_cell.py`: the frozen tasks plus the 8 backward
   tasks, x (`prism_full`, `p3_R1_A0@8k`, `p3_R1_A1n@8k`, `prism_oracle`) x 10 seeds = 1,600 cells
   for 32 forward tasks. Then `p3_stats.py --phase P3b`, extended with the bootstrap rule above.

### P3b authoring tooling (approved decisions in force)

- **Draw** (`p3b_draw.py`, committed `40f0c5f`): seed 20261024, 24 specs per corpus in draw order
  (`benchmarks/layer4/specs_p3b.py`, generated, never edited by hand; the full order and the
  no-test-mention skips in `benchmarks/layer4_p3b/draw.json`). The corpora are Django, FastAPI,
  Express and tRPC (there is no Go corpus).
- **Eligibility** (`author.py --specs benchmarks/layer4/specs_p3b.py --eligibility p3b --corpus C
  --out benchmarks/layer4_p3b`): per corpus, specs in `draw_rank` order. Each is traced and kept when the
  trace exits 0, the focal runs and 3..15 gold callers lie within `max_depth` 2. Until 8 are kept,
  each ineligible spec is replaced by the next in draw order. Every decision (kept with its gold
  count, or skipped with a reason code - `trace_exit_nonzero`, `trace_timeout`, `focal_not_run`,
  `gold_below_min`, `gold_above_max`, `not_indexed` - and the trace diagnostics) goes to
  `authoring_log_<corpus>.json` as it is made, so authoring resumes without re-tracing a decided
  spec. Any other failure (an invalid task, an unknown error) is a tooling fault: authoring
  aborts and logs nothing for that spec, so a bug never skips a draw.
- **Natural yield** (approved): a corpus whose 24 specs give fewer than 8 eligible tasks keeps what
  it has; no backfill from another corpus. Express (53 candidates) is the likely short corpus. If the
  total is below 24, the specs are extended from the same draw (`p3b_draw --n`), never redrawn.
- **Freeze** (`p3b_freeze.py freeze`): writes `benchmarks/layer4_p3b/FROZEN.json` with:
  - the seed and the sha256 of `draw.json`, `specs_p3b.py` and each authoring log;
  - per task, the sha256 of its task file and trace manifest, plus the focal, draw rank and gold count;
  - kept, trace-skipped and draw-skipped counts per corpus, and the short corpora;
  - every skipped candidate: draw skips and trace skips, with reasons.

  It refuses to re-freeze, to freeze when kept decisions and task files differ, and to freeze below 24 tasks.
- **Guard** (`p3b_freeze.verify`, called by `runner.load_tasks`): a task directory holding `p3b_*`
  tasks or sitting next to a `FROZEN.json` is loaded only when every task file and trace manifest
  matches its frozen hash and no task is missing or extra. Any P3b cell inherits this; the cell
  also runs `p3b_freeze verify` explicitly before any model call.
- **Kaggle** (`kaggle/p3b_authoring_cell.py`, CPU only):
  - builds the corpora's test environments as the tournament cell does;
  - authors in two lanes (django + trpc, fastapi + express);
  - pushes `benchmarks/layer4_p3b/` every 10 minutes and after each corpus;
  - then freezes and verifies, and writes `AUTHORING.md`.

  Decisions resume only across identical authoring code (tree hashes of `src/` and `harness/`,
  and the draw and specs files).

## Superseded P3b draft (before P3a; kept for the record)

Replaced by the P3b blueprint below: P3a showed R0_A0 is weaker than the shipped two-pass
`prism_full`, and that 4-axis profiles add nothing.

- Unit: a task. Its score per arm = mean forward F1 over 10 seeds. Comparisons are two-sided exact
  sign tests over tasks (ties dropped), with the mean F1 difference reported.
- Model: `qwen2.5-coder:14b-instruct-q8_0`, Ollama, `num_ctx` 24576, the tournament's sampler
  settings, seeds 42-51.
- Fixed-sequence hierarchy at 8k, each step at alpha = 0.05, stopping at the first failure. This
  controls the family-wise error without splitting alpha five ways.
  1. **Reach**: R1_A0 beats R0_A0.
  2. **Compression**: R1_A1n is not worse than R1_A0 - mean F1 at most 0.02 lower, and not
     significantly worse.
  3. **Profiles**: R1_A5 beats R1_A1n.
- The winner is the last arm whose step passed (R0_A0 if step 1 fails). R1_A4 vs R1_A0 and every
  4k comparison are reported as diagnostics. A fabrication rate more than 0.05 above the
  baseline's disqualifies an arm.
- Shipping the winner means implementing its reach and representation in the production packer
  and renderer, with their own tests. P3 does not ship code.
