- No summaries are used, so P3 needs no summarization run.

## Grading

- **Forward blast radius (primary).** The tournament's `score_forward`: precision, recall and F1 of
  the affected functions the model names, against runtime-traced callers (Django: tables and
  side-effect tags too). It discriminates: in the tournament, mean F1 was 0.07 with no context,
  0.50 for `prism_full` and 0.78 with an oracle context.
- **Fabrication (secondary).** Named symbols that are not in the corpus index at all - the
  hallucination measure, from the same reply.
- **Backward contract adaptation (reported, not decisive).** Splice the reply and run the corpus's
  gating tests. In the tournament every arm, the oracle included, passed 0-3% (0 of 80 with oracle
  context): a floor effect at this model size. It cannot rank five arms.
- **Q2E grounding (deferred).** Its grader counts a claim as supported only when a full caller body
  showing the call was delivered, so it penalizes signature-compressed arms by construction. It also
  grades stored T2/T5 answers, which P3 would first have to generate. A fair version needs a rule
  for compressed evidence, decided before any P3 answer exists.

## The task-set problem

The 16 Layer 4 tasks (8 forward, 8 backward; Django, FastAPI, Express, tRPC) are what P3 can run
on today, but:
- only the 8 forward tasks can discriminate;
- the 4 Python forward tasks are among the 29 cases P1 used to design R1, so they are not held
  out for R1;
- 8 tasks are few: a two-sided sign test over tasks needs 7 / 1 to reach p < 0.05.

Proposal:

- **P3a, exploratory**: the 16 existing tasks x 10 arms x 10 seeds (1,600 cells). It reports
  everything and decides nothing.
- **P3b, confirmatory**: at least 24 new forward tasks, authored with `layer4/author.py` and traced
  on fresh focal symbols across the four corpora, frozen before any P3 cell runs. The rule below
  applies to P3b only.

## Pre-registered rule (draft, for P3b)

- Unit: a task. Its score per arm = mean forward F1 over 10 seeds. Comparisons are two-sided exact
  sign tests over tasks (ties dropped), with the mean F1 difference reported.
- Model: `qwen2.5-coder:14b-instruct-q8_0`, Ollama, `num_ctx` 24576, the tournament's sampler
  settings, seeds 42-51.
- Fixed-sequence hierarchy at 8k, each step at alpha = 0.05, stopping at the first failure. This
  controls the family-wise error without splitting alpha five ways.
  1. **Reach**: R1_A0 beats R0_A0.
  2. **Compression**: R1_A1n is not worse than R1_A0 - mean F1 at most 0.02 lower, and not
     significantly worse.
  3. **Profiles**: R1_A5 beats R1_A1n.
- The winner is the last arm whose step passed (R0_A0 if step 1 fails). R1_A4 vs R1_A0 and every
  4k comparison are reported as diagnostics. A fabrication rate more than 0.05 above the
  baseline's disqualifies an arm.
- Shipping the winner means implementing its reach and representation in the production packer
  and renderer, with their own tests. P3 does not ship code.

## P3a tooling

- `p3_stats.py RUN_DIR --phase P3a`: per-arm forward F1 / precision / recall, fabrication rate
  (names not in the corpus index, over all names), context tokens, backward pass rate; the 10 x 10
  win/loss matrix on per-task mean F1; the fixed-sequence rule with the fabrication disqualifier.
  On P3a it is labelled a diagnostic and decides nothing.
- `kaggle/p3a_cell.py`: the tournament cell, with this branch, `PUSH_PATH`
  `reports/retrieval_pivot/p3a`, no contamination step, `runner run --arms p3`, then merge and
  `p3_stats`. 16 tasks x 10 arms x 10 seeds = 1,600 cells; resumable across sessions.
- Dry run of all 160 contexts (16 tasks x 10 arms; `reports/retrieval_pivot/p3/p3_dryrun_*.json`):
  no leaks, the focal in every context, every context within its budget. 12 contexts were trimmed
  by 16 nodes, because the joined text tokenizes slightly larger than the sum of node costs. Gold
  callers delivered on the 8 forward tasks, of 63:

  | | R0_A0 | R1_A0 | R1_A1n | R1_A4 | R1_A5 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 4k | 19 | 34 | 39 | 34 | 38 |
  | 8k | 20 | 45 | 47 | 45 | 46 |
